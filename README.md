# Prakob — ประกอบ

**From an idea to a website, with less infrastructure to worry about.**

We're building [Prakob](https://prakob.app/), an AI-assisted
website creation platform for people who want to bring an idea
online without having to become developers or server administrators.

Our goal is to bring website creation, editing and managed hosting
into one straightforward workflow.

## Start with your idea

A business website, a portfolio, an online shop or a booking system —
every project starts with explaining what you want to build.

The current public preview lets you explore examples, draft a
website idea and download the outline. It is an early, rule-based
demo; AI website generation and managed hosting are still in development.

**[Explore Prakob →](https://prakob.app/)**
